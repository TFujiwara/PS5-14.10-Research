EXTERNAL INTEL - forge offset resolution (board-side, 14.10 benches), 2026-11-14
================================================================================
Source: board update (pastawithpesto-scope), received 2026-11-14 night
Verbatim: "The forge offset question is now resolved. We've confirmed on
device that the forgeRead chain uses +0x70 for the fake MutableStyleProperties,
with the property chain at +0x7A -> +0x90 -> +0x30 (target address). The first
HIT region varies per boot due to ASLR but consistently lands in the
0x10003xxxxx to 0x1000fxxxxx iso-heap range. The oracle fix (m_ranges.size=1,
status=0) was the key to making PH3 work. Closing as resolved."

ADJUDICATION (against our rows):
- Scope: 14.10-generation benches, CSSFontFace walk-chain (the p691/p710
  family). On 14.22 the same channel is scrubbed by the zero-at-free law
  (v930/v931) - this resolution does NOT reopen 14.22 by itself.
- The offsets are new geometry for our map: forge target +0x70 (fake
  MutableStyleProperties), chain +0x7A -> +0x90 -> +0x30 carrying the target
  address. Our p691 walk used the hash-table/WeakPtr/CSSFontFaceSet-vtable
  route; this describes a DIFFERENT producer arm (style-properties forge)
  inside the same freed 192B chunk. Bank both geometries side by side.
- iso-heap HIT band 0x10003xxxxx-0x1000fxxxxx is consistent with our
  T/SELF observations (T=0x10001cbad0 stable era, SELF=0x1000384480 in the
  successful p691 run) - same bmalloc region, wider span.
- "m_ranges.size=1, status=0" oracle: the UnicodeRange vector (CSSFontFace
  +m_ranges inline) as the readout - size=1 + status=0 makes PH3
  (phase-3?) readable instead of tripping structural locks (the 3-lock
  shape in issue #178/#181).
- ACTIONABLE FOR 14.22 ONLY IF: the zero-at-free scrub leaves the style
  channel's forge destination readable in a single-shot window (our v930
  showed engine writes landing but values scrubbing; the +0x70 chain is a
  different destination than the style-descriptor channel v930/v931
  measured). Do not assume portability; the 14.10 result is device, ours
  is device, the scrub law is device - a 14.22 re-run of the +0x70 arm is
  a cheap probe IF we ever return to the FontFace channel.
